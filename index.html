My Favourite Persons

~ Rohit sharma

Rohit Sharma is more than just a cricketer; he is an emotion for millions of fans. Rohit Sharma is a celebrated Indian international cricketer, renowned for his explosive batting and leadership. Born on April 30, 1987, in Bansod, Nagpur, Maharashtra, he is currently the captain of the Indian cricket team in One-Day Internationals and has previously led the team in T20Is and Tests. Nicknamed the “Hitman,” Sharma is the only player in history to have scored three double centuries in ODI cricket and holds the record for the highest individual ODI score (264 runs). He has also led India to major tournament victories, including the 2024 T20 World Cup, and captained the Mumbai Indians to five IPL titles, making him one of the most successful leaders in franchise cricket. Sharma’s achievements have earned him top sporting honors like the Arjuna Award and Khel Ratna Award.Highest Individual Score in ODIs: Rohit holds the world record for the highest individual score in One-Day Internationals, smashing 264 runs against Sri Lanka in 2014 at Eden Gardens—a feat that remains unbeaten and is celebrated as one of the most extraordinary innings in cricket history. Only Player with Three ODI Double Centuries: He is the only cricketer to have scored three double centuries in ODIs, a unique achievement that underscores his dominance in the format.


~ Dishuuu (Mera pyar)

Dishuuu — simple, cute, and beautiful. A girl from Madhya Pradesh — we met online through an app called Sherchat. It started with just a few casual conversations, but in no time, those talks turned into feelings, and those feelings became something we both couldn't ignore. Slowly, unknowingly, that bond turned into love — real, deep, and soulful love. For five years, we were together. Through happiness, fights, silence, laughter, and tears — we stayed. Many problems came our way, times when everything felt like it was falling apart, but we fought for each other. We didn't give up. We kept choosing each other, again and again, no matter how hard life made it. But in the end... she got married. On 18th April 2025, she became someone else’s forever. I know I’m not that smart, not that handsome — not someone any girl would usually fall in love with... but she did. Dishuuu is that part of my life who brings color to every moment. Her smile is the light in my darkest days, and her presence has become the strength behind my every weakness. When the world chose to ignore me, she chose to understand me. Every word she speaks, and even her silence, holds a feeling that only the heart can understand. Maybe I was never good enough for her, but she never made me feel that way. She is not just a memory — she is a feeling, a heartbeat I carry deep within me every single day. We didn’t meet in some perfect moment. We met unexpectedly — like two strangers crossing paths meant to find each other. I don’t even know what pulled us closer — a message, a glance, a conversation… but whatever it was, it changed everything for me. From a simple “hi” to late-night talks, from comfort to connection, she became the chapter of my story I never knew I was waiting for.



~ Cillian Murphy (Actor)

Cillian Murphy is an acclaimed Irish actor known for his intense performances and versatile roles across film, television, and stage. Born on May 25, 1976, in Douglas, County Cork, Ireland, Murphy initially pursued a career in law before turning to acting. His breakthrough came with the 2002 film 28 Days Later, where his portrayal of Jim, a survivor in a post-apocalyptic world, earned him international recognition. Murphy is perhaps best known for his role as Thomas Shelby in the hit television series Peaky Blinders. His portrayal of the complex, charismatic gangster leader has been widely praised for its depth and nuance, making the character iconic in contemporary television. Beyond Peaky Blinders, Murphy has worked with renowned directors like Christopher Nolan, starring in films such as Inception, The Dark Knight trilogy, and Dunkirk. His ability to embody diverse characters—from a mysterious hitman in Inception to a tormented soldier in Dunkirk—showcases his remarkable range. Known for his piercing blue eyes and distinctive features, Murphy combines a quiet intensity with emotional vulnerability, captivating audiences worldwide. Off-screen, he maintains a private life, focusing on his family and music interests. Cillian Murphy continues to be a powerful presence in cinema, admired for his dedication to craft and compelling screen presence.


~ Arijit Singh (Singer)

Arijit Singh, born on 25 April 1987 in Jiaganj, Murshidabad, West Bengal, is one of India’s most celebrated playback singers and music composers. Raised in a musically inclined family—his father a Punjabi Sikh and his mother a Bengali Hindu—Arijit was exposed to both classical and contemporary music from an early age. He trained under several gurus, learning Indian classical, tabla, and Rabindra Sangeet. Singh’s career began in 2005 when he participated in the reality show Fame Gurukul at age 18, reaching the finals but finishing sixth. He later won another reality show, 10 Ke 10 Le Gaye Dil, and worked as a music programmer with prominent composers before debuting as a playback singer with “Phir Mohabbat” in Murder 2 (2011). His breakthrough came with “Tum Hi Ho” from Aashiqui 2 (2013), earning him his first Filmfare Award. Arijit has since won numerous accolades, including two National Film Awards, seven Filmfare Awards, and the Padma Shri in 2025. He is known for his soulful voice, versatility, and humility, and is the most-followed artist globally on Spotify as of 2025. Despite his fame, Arijit remains grounded, focusing on music, family, and philanthropy.





~ Zakir Hussain (Tabla Maestro)

Ustad Zakir Hussain (1951–2024) was universally acclaimed as the world’s greatest tabla virtuoso and a transformative figure in Indian classical and world music. Born in Mumbai to the legendary tabla maestro Ustad Alla Rakha, Zakir was immersed in rhythm from infancy—his father began teaching him tabla patterns almost as soon as he could sit upright. By age seven, Zakir was performing on stage, and by twelve, he was touring internationally, including collaborations with sitar maestro Pandit Ravi Shankar. Zakir Hussain’s artistry redefined the role of the tabla, elevating it from a supporting instrument to a central, expressive force in both solo and collaborative performances. His technical mastery, speed, and creativity became legendary, with critics marveling at the “blur of his fingers” and his ability to infuse percussion with melody and emotion. He was a pioneer in blending Indian classical music with global genres, collaborating with Western musicians and creating groundbreaking fusion works that introduced the tabla to new audiences worldwide. Throughout his nearly six-decade career, Hussain received numerous honors, including the Padma Shri (1988), Padma Bhushan (2002), and the Sangeet Natak Akademi Award. Internationally, he was awarded the United States’ National Heritage Fellowship and won four Grammy Awards, three of them in 2024 alone. Hussain also contributed to film music, acted in several movies, and was the first Indian musician invited to the White House for the All-Star Global Concert in 2016.
